Privacy Policy — Achievement Journal

Last updated October 5, 2026

Achievement Journal (formerly Technology Greatness Journal) is an iPhone app published by Gordon Liang (“we,” “us,” or “our”). It is a journaling app: your days are matched with the real durations of great human accomplishments in technology, so you can write and stay inspired.

Everything you write and everything you tell the app stays on your iPhone. There is no account, no server, and no cloud service behind this app. We cannot read your journal entries.

1. What the app stores

Achievement Journal stores the following on your device:

  • your stated intention and the mode you chose (reflection or progress);
  • the reasons you selected for writing against that intention;
  • the category of accomplishments and the image theme you chose;
  • the start date of each endeavour;
  • the text of your journal entries, including entries for days with or without a matching accomplishment; and
  • how often you journaled before using this app, which you may provide during onboarding.

Prior journaling frequency is used only to personalize onboarding. It is not transmitted anywhere. The app does not ask for your name or age range, and it does not use a subscription.

2. How the information is used

The information above is used only to run the app on your device: to build your journal prompt from the wording you entered, and to display your galleries in the theme you chose.

It is not used for advertising, profiling, tracking, or machine-learning training. We do not sell, rent, license, or share it, because we never receive it.

3. Storage and security

All app data is stored locally on your iPhone using iOS’s standard app preference storage, inside the app’s own container and protected by iOS data protection and your device passcode.

Achievement Journal has no user accounts, no backend server, and no cloud sync of its own. Your device backups (including iCloud Backup if you use it) may include the app’s data as part of a normal device backup. Nothing you write is uploaded to us or to any server we operate.

4. Notifications

The current version of Achievement Journal does not schedule notifications and does not use push notifications. No push token or device identifier is transmitted to us.

5. Third parties

Apple is the only third party involved in distributing the app through the App Store. We never see or handle your payment-card, billing, or Apple Account details.

Achievement Journal contains no analytics SDKs, no advertising SDKs, and no third-party trackers. It does not use cookies or any form of web tracking. We do not sell or share personal information.

6. Sharing and export

The app has no sharing features, no export, and no social features. Your journal entries stay in the app on your device.

7. Children

Achievement Journal is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Because all information stays on the device, a parent or guardian can remove everything the app has stored by deleting the app.

8. Retention and deletion

Your entries and past galleries are kept on your device indefinitely so that you can return to them. The app does not expire or automatically remove anything.

You control deletion:

  • delete an individual entry from a gallery — this is permanent and cannot be undone; or
  • delete the app from your iPhone, which removes all data Achievement Journal has stored (aside from any copies already in your own device backups).

Because we never receive your information, we hold no copy of it and cannot retrieve or delete it for you.

9. Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or obtain a copy of your personal information.

Everything Achievement Journal stores about you is on your own device, where you can read it, edit it, and delete it directly. We cannot provide, correct, or delete information that we do not receive and do not hold.

If you email us, we receive your email address and the contents of your message. We retain that information only as reasonably necessary to respond and maintain appropriate business records.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the date at the top.

If we materially change how information is collected, used, or stored — for example, if a future version adds sync or any other off-device feature — we will provide notice in the app before the change takes effect.
