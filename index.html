    Private by design

    Your journal never leaves your iPhone.

    • No account. No sign-up, no email, no password to open the app.
    • No server. Entries are stored on your phone, protected by your passcode.
    • No tracking. No analytics, no ad SDKs, no third-party trackers.
    • No notifications. It waits for you. It never nags.

    Questions

    When can I get it?

    The free iPhone beta runs on TestFlight, Apple's beta app. Leave your email and we'll send you the link as soon as it opens.

    What does it cost?

    The beta is free. At launch the app is free to download, with an optional one-time upgrade, Plus Lifetime: $39.99, or a $29.99 founding price at launch. Pay once. No subscription.

    Do I have to write every day?

    No. Write one when it strikes you, or none for a month. Nothing expires and nothing is missed. There are no streaks to break.

    Which phones?

    iPhone, on iOS 18 or later. There's no Android version right now.

    Where do the photos come from?

    Public-domain photographs and artwork from NASA and the U.S. Department of Defense, found through Wikimedia Commons. Full credits are at the bottom of this page.

    What happens to my email?

    It goes to Gordon, the developer, through FormSubmit, a form-to-email service. It's used only to send you the beta link and launch news. Reply "stop" to any email and you're off the list.
